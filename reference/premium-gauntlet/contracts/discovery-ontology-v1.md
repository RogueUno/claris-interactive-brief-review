# CLARIS Discovery Intelligence Ontology V1

The ontology is the complete internal universe CLARIS may consider. It is not a questionnaire. Only authority-gated, unresolved, decision-relevant dimensions surface on a call plan.

- D01 conversation_reason — explicit subject/problem/outcome brought to the call
- D02 technical_or_service_scope — exact system, control boundary, workload, framework, service surface
- D03 current_state — relevant existing controls/process/architecture/tooling/certifications
- D04 problem_or_gap — what is failing, uncertain, manual, blocked or unsatisfactory
- D05 implication — operational/commercial/security consequence if the confirmed problem remains
- D06 desired_outcome — result, decision, artifact or change wanted
- D07 prior_attempts — what has already been tried, assessed or bought
- D08 constraints_dependencies — technical, regulatory, contractual or delivery constraints
- D09 ownership_stakeholders — implementation/decision/evidence owners
- D10 economics — direct budget/commercial fit evidence
- D11 timing_trigger — explicit deadline/event/why-now
- D12 decision_process — evaluation/procurement/approval criteria
- D13 assurance_requirements — customer/auditor/framework/evidence requirements
- D14 delivery_fit — assessment vs implementation vs advisory vs retainer
- D15 next_move — what must be true for the correct next step

## Authority model
A primary discovery dimension may be opened only by:
1. BOOKING evidence,
2. PROSPECT-reported evidence, or
3. explicit CONSULTANT_POLICY.

Public research may enrich an admitted dimension, suppress redundant questions, and make options more precise. Public research never opens a primary discovery dimension.

A conditional follow-up may open a deeper ontology family only when the triggering prospect answer explicitly introduces that family.
