# CLARIS Premium Brief Contract V3.6

A discovery dimension may be opened only by BOOKING evidence, PROSPECT evidence, or explicit CONSULTANT_POLICY. FIRST_PARTY_PUBLIC and THIRD_PARTY_PUBLIC research may enrich an already-open dimension but may never create one.

Every open dimension records its authority. Booking/prospect authority should preserve a literal supporting quote; policy authority cites a stable policy key.

The main brief is a dense decision memo. Provenance is expandable:
- E# Evidence: source -> admitted fact -> used_for -> not_used_for
- R# Reasoning: premises -> bounded observation -> not_a_claim_of
- U# Unknown: why unresolved -> what resolves -> blocked conclusions
